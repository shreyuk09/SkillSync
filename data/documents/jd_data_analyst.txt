Data Analyst
Meridian Retail Group | Hyderabad, India | Full-time

ABOUT US
Meridian Retail Group operates 240 stores and a growing online business. The analytics
team turns sales, inventory and customer data into the decisions that drive buying,
pricing and store operations.

WHAT YOU WILL DO
- Build and maintain dashboards that track sales, margin and inventory performance.
- Write SQL queries against our data warehouse to answer business questions.
- Clean, join and validate data from multiple source systems.
- Perform exploratory data analysis to explain changes in key metrics.
- Run and interpret A/B tests for pricing and promotion experiments.
- Present findings to category managers who are not technical.
- Automate recurring reports so the team stops rebuilding them by hand.
- Partner with the data engineering team on data quality issues.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Computer Science or a
  related quantitative field.
- Strong SQL skills, including joins, window functions and aggregation.
- Proficiency with Excel, including pivot tables and lookup functions.
- Working knowledge of Python for data analysis, particularly pandas and numpy.
- Experience building visualisations in Power BI or Tableau.
- Understanding of descriptive statistics and hypothesis testing.
- Clear written and verbal communication.

PREFERRED SKILLS
- Experience with ETL tools or building data pipelines.
- Familiarity with cloud data warehouses such as Snowflake or BigQuery.
- Exposure to Apache Airflow.
- Basic machine learning knowledge for forecasting.
- Retail or e-commerce domain experience.

KEYWORDS
SQL, Python, pandas, Power BI, Tableau, Excel, data visualisation, statistics,
A/B testing, ETL, data warehouse, dashboards, reporting, data cleaning
